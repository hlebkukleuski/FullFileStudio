Privacy Policy for FullFileStudio

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

FullFileStudio is developed by Hleb Kukleuski as an open-source, privacy-first mobile file reader. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to protecting your privacy.

1. Data Collection & Processing

FullFileStudio operates completely offline for document reading, OCR recognition, media playback, and database inspection. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, documents, telemetry, or user content.

2. Permissions

FullFileStudio requests Android system permissions solely to access files selected directly by the user via the Storage Access Framework (SAF) or file picker. Permissions are never used for background data collection.

3. Third-Party Services

The Google Play distribution of the app may use Google Play Dynamic Delivery to install optional format modules on demand. No personal identifiers are transmitted during module installation.
